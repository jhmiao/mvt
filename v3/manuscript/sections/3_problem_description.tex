
\section{Problem Description}
There are two types of nurses: Registered Nurses (RNs) and Licensed Vocational Nurses (LVNs). Each event has a fixed date, known location, known duration, and associated required number of RNs and LVNs. For each event, one leader will be selected to pick up and drop off the vaccines and equipments at the depot, as the first and the last thing in the day. The planning horizon is one week, where we need to assign event to time slots, assign nurses to events, and pick the leader for each event. The objective is to minimize the travel cost and ensure fairness across nurses over time.
